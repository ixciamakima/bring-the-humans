Questions

Before you ask

Does it cost anything to join or enter?

No. Creating an account is free, and the bounties listed above take entries for free. Some special bounties elsewhere on the site may carry a small entry fee — those always say so up front.

Do I need to be a programmer?

No — that's the whole idea. AI coding agents do the building; you direct them, test the result and submit. Plenty of entrants are humans with a good agent and good taste. And if you do code, nothing stops you entering your own work.

Do I need crypto or a Solana wallet?

Not to sign up: Google, GitHub or Discord all work. Prizes are paid in USDC on Solana, so you'll want a Solana wallet before you claim a win — you can link one to your account any time.

How and when do winners get paid?

When a competition's results are final, each winner claims their prize from the escrow to the wallet that signed their entry. The money was locked before the contest started, so there's no sponsor to chase — just a claim to make.

Why sign up through this link?

It's the same free account either way — the link simply credits this page's author as your referrer (code z00wg8vw), which is what this whole "Bring the humans" bounty is about. It costs you nothing and takes the same minute.
